Desk Arcade

Mini-games that play on top of your desktop while a build, a deploy or a coding agent works. Clicks go straight through to your windows, and window tops are the platforms.

Download Desk Arcade

Free and open source · Windows, Linux and macOS

Sixty games, over your windows

Hoops on your window tops, pinball with the taskbar as the drain, chess and checkers, solitaire, Durak and Poker with co-workers, falling blocks, Load Balancer, Spot the Bug and the rest. Somebody to play against in every one: a computer rival at four levels, or the person at the next desk over the local network.

Made for the time you wait

  • Play while it builds

    arcade dotnet test runs your command as usual, shows it on the scoreboard and chimes when it passes or fails.

  • Coding agents

    Claude Code, Codex, Aider and Cursor show up on the scoreboard, one line per session: working, done, or needs you.

  • CI and pull requests

    Your branch's CI run and the reviews waiting for you, through the gh or glab you already signed in to.

  • VS Code

    An extension lights a lane for every task and debug session, and a button in the status bar shows or hides the games.

  • Status lanes

    deskarcade --status deploy running from any script, git hook or watch task.

  • The wait report

    How long builds, tests, CI and agents kept you waiting this week, and which are getting slower.

Help with the working day

  • Steps aside

    The games hide by themselves for a presentation or a full-screen app.

  • Meetings

    A heads-up from your calendar with a Join button for Teams, Zoom or Meet, and the free stretches in between offered as focus blocks.

  • Focus

    Focus blocks with the games as the break, brown noise or rain to work to, and knocks that wait for your break.

  • Breaks

    Rest your eyes, stretch and drink water, by time at the computer.

  • Morning and evening

    Standup notes from yesterday's commits, today's three things, and a word about unpushed work before you go.

  • Coffee

    Invite co-workers for coffee, lunch or a walk, with a card on their screen.

Better with the office

Quiz Night, Draw & Guess, Poker and Hearts for the whole team on the local network; duels, races and board games for two; a record against each co-worker, a monthly ladder and an office leaderboard. Nothing goes beyond your network.

Get it

Windows

The installer (per user, no admin), or:

winget install ImperiumGames.DeskArcade
scoop bucket add deskarcade https://github.com/BokhodirUrinboev/scoop-bucket
scoop install deskarcade/deskarcade
choco install deskarcade

Linux

The .deb or AppImage, or on Arch:

yay -S deskarcade-bin

macOS

Experimental, not signed by Apple: right-click → Open the first time.

brew install --cask bokhodirurinboev/tap/deskarcade

Everything stays on your computer. No account, no telemetry. The only things that use the network are the ones you turn on: playing with co-workers on your local network, the office leaderboard, your calendar link, and a daily check for updates.
